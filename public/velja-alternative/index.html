A free alternative to Velja

Updated 17 November 2026

Velja by Sindre Sorhus is a great browser picker, and if it works for you, keep using it. Detour does the same job in a different way: it learns where your links belong from your own browsing history, so you write fewer rules and get asked less. It's free and open source.

Where Velja is better

Velja is more mature and polished, and its browser extensions catch links you click inside a browser, which Detour can't do yet. If you mostly click links inside your browser, Velja is the better pick today.

Where Detour is better

Most browser pickers, Velja included, decide by rules you write and ask when no rule fits. Detour also looks at where you opened each site before, per profile, per app you clicked in and per workspace (github.com/your-company versus github.com/you). In practice that means most links need no rule at all. Settings show you why each link went where it did.

Switching from Velja

Install Detour, run the two-minute setup and make it your default browser. Setup finds your browsers and profiles and suggests rules from your history, so you don't have to copy your Velja rules by hand. To go back, pick Velja as your default browser again.
