Every link opens in the right browser and profile.

Detour is a free browser picker for Mac that decides for you. Work links open in your work Chrome profile, video in your ad-free browser, everything else where it belongs. It only asks when it really can't tell.

Browser pickers ask you every time you click. Detour learns where your links belong, and tells you why.

How Detour decides where a link opens

For every link it runs through these checks, in this order, and stops at the first clear answer.

  1. Your rules

    Sites you always want in one place, like your company's tools in the work profile or YouTube in Firefox.

  2. Google accounts

    Calendar invites carry the owner's email, and Docs you opened before are remembered per profile.

  3. What you taught it

    Moved a link to another profile from the menu bar? Detour remembers that site.

  4. Your history

    If you open a site in one profile nine times out of ten, that's where it goes. It even tells github.com/your-company apart from github.com/you. No rule needed.

  5. Where you clicked

    Send links from Slack to work and from Messages to personal, or let Detour learn that from your own clicks.

  6. Ask

    Still unsure? A small picker appears at your cursor. Press 1, 2 or 3. Hold ⌥ to always choose yourself.

Open links in a specific Chrome profile, set up in two minutes

Detour finds your browsers and profiles by itself, including Chrome, Brave, Edge and Vivaldi profiles. Name them, pick a default, and it suggests rules from your own history: the sites you already open in the same place every time.

  • Chrome, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi and Chromium profiles
  • Firefox, Safari, Arc and others as a whole browser
  • Learns from Chrome, Brave, Edge, Firefox and Safari history
  • Recent links in the menu bar, with "Open in…" to correct and teach
  • Test any link in settings to see where it goes, and why
  • Removes tracking parameters and can open Zoom, Teams and Slack links in their apps

Private by design

No tracking. Detour opens links and makes no requests of its own. The only exception is an update check you can turn on, which asks GitHub for the latest version once a day.

A summary, not a copy. To learn, it counts per site how often you visited it in each profile. Full URLs and page titles are not stored.

No account. Settings are a plain file on your Mac that you can read and edit.

Open source. MIT licensed, so every line of what it does is public.

Install Detour

Download

Open Detour.dmg and drag Detour to Applications. Signed and notarized by Apple.

Homebrew

brew install --cask ten50-nl/tap/detour, updates with brew upgrade.

From source

Clone the repository and run scripts/build-app.sh --install. Needs Xcode 16.

Questions

How do I open links in a specific Chrome profile on Mac?

Install Detour and make it your default browser during setup. It sends each link to the Chrome, Brave, Edge or Vivaldi profile you choose, based on your rules and where you opened that site before.

Is it really free?

Yes. Detour is MIT licensed, with no paid version, no account and no tracking, and it will stay that way. ten50 builds it because we use it every day.

Why does it need Full Disk Access?

macOS keeps browser profiles and history behind that permission. Detour reads them on your Mac to show your profiles and to learn where you open each site. You can skip it; Detour then routes to whole browsers only.

Can it open a specific Firefox or Safari profile?

Not yet. Firefox can't reliably open a link in another profile while it's running, and Safari profiles can't be reached from outside. Both work as a whole browser.

Does it replace my browser?

No. macOS sees Detour as your default browser, but it only passes each link on to one of your real browsers. Nothing about them changes.

How do I undo it?

Choose another default browser in System Settings, quit Detour from the menu bar and delete it. Settings live in ~/Library/Application Support/Detour if you want to remove those too.
