Every link opens in the right browser and profile.

Detour is a small Mac app that sits between your apps and your browsers. Work links go to your work profile, video goes to your ad-free browser, and it only asks when it really can't tell.

Download for macOS macOS 15 or later
brew install --cask ten50nl/tap/detour

Free and open source, forever. No account, no tracking.

Browser pickers ask you every time you click. Detour decides for you, and tells you why.

How Detour decides

For every link it walks through these checks and stops at the first clear answer.

  1. Your rules

    Sites you always want in one place, like your company's tools in the work profile or YouTube in Firefox.

  2. Google links

    Calendar invites carry the owner's email, and Docs you opened before are remembered per profile. Your work invite opens where your work account lives.

  3. Choices you taught it

    Moved a link to another profile from the menu bar? Detour remembers that site.

  4. Your history

    If you open a site in one profile nine times out of ten, that's where it goes. No rule needed.

  5. Where you clicked

    A link from Slack can lean towards work, one from WhatsApp towards personal.

  6. Ask

    Still not sure, like a Google Doc nobody opened before? A small picker appears at your cursor. Press 1, 2 or 3.

Hold ⌥ while clicking any link to choose the destination yourself.

Set up in two minutes

Detour finds your browsers and profiles by itself, including Chrome, Brave, Edge and Vivaldi profiles. Name them, pick a default, and it suggests rules from your own history: the sites you already open in the same place every time.

  • Works with Chrome, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi and Chromium profiles
  • Firefox, Safari, Arc and others as a whole browser
  • Up to nine destinations
  • Recent links in the menu bar, with "Open in…" to correct and teach

Your links stay yours

No network. Detour opens links. It makes no requests of its own, so there is nothing to track.

Local history summary. To learn, it counts per site how often you visited it in each profile. Full URLs and page titles are not stored.

No account. Settings are a plain file on your Mac you can read and edit.

Open source. MIT licensed, so you can read every line of what it does.

Install

Download

Open Detour.dmg and drag Detour to Applications. It's signed and notarized by Apple.

Homebrew

brew install --cask ten50nl/tap/detour

Updates come with brew upgrade.

From source

git clone https://github.com/ten50nl/detour and run scripts/build-app.sh --install. Needs Xcode 16.

Requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Detour is MIT licensed, with no paid version, no account and no tracking, and it will stay that way. ten50 builds it because we use it every day.

Why does it need Full Disk Access?

macOS keeps browser profiles and history behind that permission. Detour reads them on your Mac to show your profiles and to learn where you open each site. You can skip it; Detour then routes to whole browsers only.

Can it open a specific Firefox or Safari profile?

Not yet. Firefox can't reliably open a link in another profile while it's running, and Safari profiles can't be reached from outside. Both work as a whole browser.

Does it replace my browser?

No. macOS sees Detour as your default browser, but it only passes each link on to one of your real browsers. Nothing about them changes.

How do I undo it?

Choose another default browser in System Settings, quit Detour from the menu bar and delete it. Your settings live in ~/Library/Application Support/Detour if you want to remove those too.

Where do I report a bug?

On GitHub. Pull requests are welcome too.
